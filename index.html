Practical, Not Hype

How I Use AI

At Work

I use AI to troubleshoot complex issues, write and review PowerShell, and draft documentation and SOPs. I research configurations with it before I change anything, and I train staff to use AI tools well and safely.

In My Home Lab

At home I run agentic AI workflows on macOS and build apps, websites, and scripts with AI-assisted development. It's where I try new tools before they get anywhere near production.

My Principles

AI extends my expertise. It doesn't replace it. I check AI-written code and advice, protect security and privacy, and keep unverified output away from critical systems.
